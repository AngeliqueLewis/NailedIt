NailedIT 💅✨
Welcome to NailedIT — the ultimate app to help you decide on the perfect nail color and design in a fun, girly way! Whether you're having a hard time choosing between colors or just want a bit of creative inspiration, NailedIT has got you covered!

🚀 Features
Nail Color Picker: Choose from a wide range of beautiful colors to match your mood or outfit.

Design Inspiration: Get playful with various fun nail designs to make your nails stand out.

Randomized Picker: Not sure what to choose? Let the app randomly decide your nail design for you!

User-Friendly Interface: Simple, intuitive, and totally girly – just tap, choose, and glam up your nails!

View it LIVE here -->https://younailedit.netlify.app/

💡 How It Works
Open the app and navigate to the Nail Color section.

Select your preferred color (or let the app choose for you!).

Browse through unique and fun nail design options.

Once you’ve picked your favorite, save your design and get ready to show it off!

🛠️ Technologies Used
HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Core Data for saving and retrieving your chosen designs.

UIKit for smooth interactions and a fun experience.
